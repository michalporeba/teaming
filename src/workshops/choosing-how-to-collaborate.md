## Objectives

By the end, participants should be able to:

- Choose a way of working — ensemble, pairs, individual investigation or bounded parallel work — to suit the task.
- Keep shared ownership and useful integration while working apart.
- Recognise when a change crosses agreed boundaries and the team needs to regroup.

> The advanced skill is not maintaining a mob at all costs. It is preserving shared ownership while changing how the work is organized.

## How to run it

This challenges the assumption that the most mature response is always to keep everyone in one synchronous session.

### Before the session

- A task with well-specified, independently testable parts and an agreed integration contract.
- A prepared later requirement that crosses those boundaries or exposes incompatible assumptions.

Candidate exercises include independent [Boober](/games/boober/) policy modules against a stable interface, or separate gift-recipe validators followed by a shared scheduling change.

### Running it

1. Let the team work on the task as one ensemble.
2. Invite them to consider separating bounded work, then reconvening to integrate.
3. Introduce the later requirement. The team decides whether to regroup, pair, investigate individually or carry on separately.
4. Compare valid delivery, participation, understanding, waiting, integration effort and response to change — not speed alone. Allow for practice effects when work is repeated.

The scenario should make splitting the work a credible and possibly better option, never a rigged one. Accept evidence that supports a different choice.
